3 What is under the hood

The engine is a JavaScript port of the Rust backtester we use for MetaTrader 5 strategies, which was validated deal-for-deal against a MetaTrader 5 Strategy Tester run. It follows the same tick loop:

RuleBehaviour
FillsBuy opens at the ask and closes at the bid; sell the opposite. The spread is whatever the ticks say.
LatencyAn order sent at t executes at t + open/close ms + jitter, at the quote valid at that moment (the previous tick if the due time falls between two ticks). Jitter uses the same seeded generator as the Rust engine.
StopsSL and TP are server-side, not delayed. An SL fills at the tick price (a gap fills worse), a TP at its level.
BarsBuilt from the bid price, only closed bars are used by the strategies, and the bars before From warm the indicators up without trading.
CostsCommission per lot and side, swap in points with the Wednesday triple rate, margin from leverage, stop-out at 50 % margin level; money is rounded to cents per deal.
DeterminismThe same data and settings always give bit-identical results.

Cross-checked. The test suite (test/backtest-lab.test.ts) pins this engine to balances, trade counts and drawdowns produced by the Rust binary for the same dataset, strategies, costs and latency settings.

Not for real decisions. The example data is a seeded random walk, not a market, and the three strategies are textbook toys. The browser version leaves out pending orders, trade-session calendars and non-USD accounts; the Rust engine supports those. Nothing here is investment advice.

Everything — parsing, bars, execution, charts — happens in engine.js and lab.js on your device. The only network requests are for the page's own files. The full Rust version, with a memory-mapped tick store and a parallel optimiser, is what this page mirrors.
