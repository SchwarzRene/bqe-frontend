Infrastructure

BQE-DeComp

A domain-specific binary codec for 1-minute OHLCV equity data. Decodes 4 million rows directly into NumPy arrays in ~9 ms using parallel lz4 decompression across independent row bands. 6.8× smaller than CSV, 2.4× faster to load than raw .npy.

Machine Learning

MarketJEPA

A self-supervised world model for the stock market — a JEPA (Joint-Embedding Predictive Architecture) trained with SIGReg on the full BQE equity universe to learn a latent representation of price action, with classifier heads and a decision policy on top that aim to generate alpha.

Interactive

HistoryMap

An interactive world-history atlas: historical borders from 9500 BC to 2010 on a time slider, with curated era reports on hegemony, wars, art & architecture and schools of thought, clickable empires, a toggleable capital-cities layer, 475+ dated chronicle entries, 220+ event markers and live Wikipedia integration. Bilingual (German/English).

Market Tape

A broadcast-style rundown for Fed events and earnings calls, built without a market-data vendor: the schedule, the live feeds and the reported numbers are all resolved at runtime by a search-grounded language model under a strict JSON contract, then validated client-side — with an on-air state machine and DST-correct exchange time in ~15 lines.
