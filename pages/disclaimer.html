Risk Warning

Trading on financial markets involves substantial risk of loss. Leveraged trading (CFDs, futures) is particularly risky. You can lose more than your initial investment. Only invest capital that you can afford to lose entirely.

CFD (Contract for Difference) - Specific Risks

CFDs carry various risks: leverage risk – CFDs use leverage, which amplifies both profits and losses – volatility risk – rapid price movements can trigger margin calls – counterparty risk – CFDs depend on the solvency of the broker – overnight gap risk – markets can gap up or down at the close – liquidity risk – some instruments may have limited liquidity.

Futures - Specific Risks

Futures contracts carry the following risks: leverage risk – futures contracts are highly leveraged – expiry risk – contracts expire and require rollovers – margin requirements – sudden margin increases can force liquidation – systemic risk – exchange closures or broker failures – basis risk – futures may not track underlying assets perfectly.
